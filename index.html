Inspired by the 1994 film "Blank Check"

How to Play

🎯 Goal: Spend your entire $1,000,000 within 7 days.

🕹️ Move (mobile): Drag the left joystick in the bottom bar.

⌨️ Move (desktop): WASD or Arrow Keys.

⚡ Sprint: Hold SPRINT button or Shift for a speed boost.

🛒 Shop: Walk near a store — SHOP button glows gold. Tap it or press E.

🗺️ Map: Toggle the MAP button to show/hide minimap.

🌡️ Heat: Big purchases raise FBI suspicion. 100% = busted!

🌙 End Day: Go to Mansion 🏰 (far bottom-right) and Sleep.

💸 Win: Drain the account before day 7 ends!
